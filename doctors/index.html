Best eye doctors in Prayagraj for diabetic patients

Diabetes is the #1 cause of blindness in adults. If you are diabetic, a dilated retina check at least once a year is a must. Here are verified retina/diabetic-eye specialists in Prayagraj.

Quick guide for diabetics

  • ◆ Annual dilated fundus (retina) exam — even if vision feels normal.
  • ◆ After a RetinaAI screening — Low risk: yearly check · Moderate: follow-up soon · High: see a retina specialist within days.
  • ◆ Sudden vision loss / many floaters / flashes → eye emergency, go immediately, don't wait.
  • ◆ Keep HbA1c, BP and cholesterol in range — that is the real medicine for diabetic eyes.
